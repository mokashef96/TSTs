The Six Pillars of Faith (Arkan al-Iman)

Read these 6 cards to become a master of the game!

1

Belief in Allah (الإيمان بالله)

Believing that Allah is One (Tawhid) with no partners. He created the universe, the skies, the earth, and all living things. Only Allah deserves our worship and prayers.

  • Allah has 99 Beautiful Names (Asma ul-Husna).
  • Al-Khaliq means The Creator; Ar-Razzaq means The Provider; Ar-Rahman means The Most Merciful.
  • Surah Al-Ikhlas teaches us about Allah's Oneness.
2

Belief in the Angels (الإيمان بالملائكة)

Angels are honored servants of Allah created from pure light (Noor). They never disobey Allah and each angel has special duties:

  • Jibril (Gabriel): Brought revelations (like the Quran) to the Prophets.
  • Mikail (Michael): In charge of rain, wind, and plants.
  • Israfil: Will blow the Trumpet on the Day of Judgment.
  • Kiraman Katibin: Noble angels who write down our good and bad deeds.
3

Belief in the Holy Books (الإيمان بالكتب)

Allah sent holy scriptures to guide humanity to truth and kindness:

  • The Holy Quran: Revealed to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ — the final book protected by Allah forever.
  • Tawrat (Torah): Given to Prophet Musa (Moses).
  • Injil (Gospel): Given to Prophet Isa (Jesus).
  • Zabur (Psalms): Given to Prophet Dawud (David).
  • Suhuf (Scrolls): Given to Prophet Ibrahim (Abraham) and Musa.
4

Belief in the Prophets (الإيمان بالرسل)

Allah chose truthful, honest role models to teach people how to worship Him and live with good manners.

  • First Prophet: Adam (AS). Final Messenger: Muhammad ﷺ.
  • 25 Prophets are mentioned by name in the Holy Quran.
  • Ulul-Azm (5 Messengers of Strong Determination): Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, Isa, and Muhammad ﷺ.
5

Day of Judgment (الإيمان باليوم الآخر)

Believing in Yawm al-Qiyamah (The Last Day), when Allah will bring everyone back to life for fair judgment.

  • Al-Mizan: The Scale of Justice where deeds are weighed.
  • Jannah (Paradise): The eternal garden of happiness for believers who do good.
  • Knowing about the Last Day encourages us to be honest, kind, and helpful every day!
6

Divine Decree / Qadar (الإيمان بالقدر)

Believing that everything happens by Allah's perfect knowledge, wisdom, and will (Al-Qada wal-Qadar).

  • Allah wrote everything in the Preserved Tablet (Al-Lawh Al-Mahfuz).
  • We work hard, make Dua, and put our trust in Allah (Tawakkul).
  • When good happens, we say "Alhamdulillah" (Shukr). When tests happen, we stay patient (Sabr).
